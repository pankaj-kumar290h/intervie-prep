# 12-Week Spoken English Plan

One quarter. By the end you should be able to speak for 2–3 minutes on a familiar
topic without long freezes, and hold a normal back-and-forth conversation.

Each day = **Daily Core** (always) + **Focus** (that week's lesson work).

- **Daily Core (~20 min):** see
  [04-speaking-practice/daily-routine.md](04-speaking-practice/daily-routine.md)
  – shadowing + self-talk + 5 new phrases reviewed.
- **Focus (~15–25 min):** the lesson reading + drills listed below.
- **6 days a week.** One rest day. Do not skip two days in a row – ever.

Log every day in [progress-tracker.md](progress-tracker.md).

---

## Phase 1 – Foundations (Weeks 1–4)

Goal: build the daily habit, fix the biggest pronunciation problems, start thinking
in English for simple things.

### Week 1 – Start the engine
- Read [04-speaking-practice/README.md](04-speaking-practice/README.md) and
  [04-speaking-practice/shadowing.md](04-speaking-practice/shadowing.md).
- Pick your one show/podcast ([06-listening/resources.md](06-listening/resources.md)).
- Read [01-pronunciation/README.md](01-pronunciation/README.md) and
  [01-pronunciation/common-mistakes-hindi-speakers.md](01-pronunciation/common-mistakes-hindi-speakers.md).
- Start [02-vocabulary/learning-system.md](02-vocabulary/learning-system.md) – set up your phrase notebook.
- **Daily:** 5 min shadowing + 10 min self-talk ("narrate my morning").

### Week 2 – Vowels and word stress
- Read [01-pronunciation/vowel-sounds.md](01-pronunciation/vowel-sounds.md).
- Read [01-pronunciation/word-stress-and-rhythm.md](01-pronunciation/word-stress-and-rhythm.md).
- Do the minimal-pairs drill in
  [01-pronunciation/minimal-pairs-drills.md](01-pronunciation/minimal-pairs-drills.md) – 5 pairs/day.
- Read [03-grammar-for-speaking/tenses-you-actually-use.md](03-grammar-for-speaking/tenses-you-actually-use.md).
- **Record #1:** describe your job for 90 seconds. Save it. Do not judge harshly.

### Week 3 – Consonants and connected speech
- Read [01-pronunciation/consonant-sounds.md](01-pronunciation/consonant-sounds.md).
- Read [01-pronunciation/connected-speech.md](01-pronunciation/connected-speech.md).
- Read [05-fluency-confidence/filler-and-buffer-phrases.md](05-fluency-confidence/filler-and-buffer-phrases.md)
  – memorize 10 buffer phrases.
- Read [02-vocabulary/core-high-frequency.md](02-vocabulary/core-high-frequency.md).
- **Daily:** self-talk now includes past tense ("what I did yesterday").

### Week 4 – Intonation + review
- Read [01-pronunciation/intonation.md](01-pronunciation/intonation.md).
- Read [03-grammar-for-speaking/articles-and-prepositions.md](03-grammar-for-speaking/articles-and-prepositions.md).
- Re-record the Week 2 "describe your job" task. Compare to Record #1.
- Month 1 self-assessment in [progress-tracker.md](progress-tracker.md).

## Phase 2 – Building fluency (Weeks 5–8)

Goal: longer stretches of speech, real conversations, bigger active vocabulary.

### Week 5 – Sentence patterns + phrasal verbs
- Read [03-grammar-for-speaking/spoken-sentence-patterns.md](03-grammar-for-speaking/spoken-sentence-patterns.md).
- Read [02-vocabulary/phrasal-verbs.md](02-vocabulary/phrasal-verbs.md) – 3/day, use each in a sentence out loud.
- Read [04-speaking-practice/monologue-topics.md](04-speaking-practice/monologue-topics.md) – 1 topic/day, 2 min each, recorded.

### Week 6 – Conversations + interview language
- Read [04-speaking-practice/conversation-scripts.md](04-speaking-practice/conversation-scripts.md) – practice both roles.
- Read [07-interview-english/explaining-technical-things.md](07-interview-english/explaining-technical-things.md).
- If possible: book 1 tutor session this week. Otherwise use a language-exchange app.

### Week 7 – Collocations + thinking in English
- Read [02-vocabulary/collocations.md](02-vocabulary/collocations.md).
- Read [05-fluency-confidence/thinking-in-english.md](05-fluency-confidence/thinking-in-english.md).
- Read [07-interview-english/behavioral-answer-language.md](07-interview-english/behavioral-answer-language.md).
- **Record #2:** answer "Tell me about yourself" (interview version), 2 min.

### Week 8 – Review + stretch
- Read [05-fluency-confidence/recovering-from-mistakes.md](05-fluency-confidence/recovering-from-mistakes.md).
- Redo 3 monologue topics from Week 5 – aim for 3 min, fewer pauses.
- Month 2 self-assessment.

---

## Phase 3 – Real-world use (Weeks 9–12)

Goal: use English in situations with stakes – meetings, interviews, calls.

### Week 9 – Speed and reduction
- Re-read [01-pronunciation/connected-speech.md](01-pronunciation/connected-speech.md) and drill it hard.
- Shadow at full speed, no pausing the audio.
- Daily self-talk → switch to **opinion** topics (see monologue-topics.md, section 3).

### Week 10 – Meetings and calls
- Read [07-interview-english/meeting-and-standup-phrases.md](07-interview-english/meeting-and-standup-phrases.md).
- Practice a mock stand-up update out loud daily.
- Do 2 tutor/exchange conversations this week if you can.

### Week 11 – Mock interviews
- Full mock interview out loud: intro + 2 behavioral + explain one project.
- Record the whole thing. Listen. Note your top 3 recurring errors.
- Targeted drilling on those 3 errors.

### Week 12 – Consolidate
- Re-record all benchmark tasks (job description, tell me about yourself, one project).
- Compare to Week 2 and Week 7 recordings. Write down what improved.
- Month 3 self-assessment. Plan the next quarter (see below).

---

## After Week 12

Keep the Daily Core forever – it becomes 15 min of maintenance. Then pick a new
focus each month: accent reduction, presentation skills, negotiation language,
small talk, writing. The habit is the asset; the topics rotate.
