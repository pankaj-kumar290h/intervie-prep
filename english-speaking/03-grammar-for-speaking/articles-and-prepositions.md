# Articles and Prepositions

The two things that never feel 100% automatic – but you can get them ~90% right with
a few solid rules and a lot of exposure. Don't aim for perfection; aim for "not
distracting."

---

## Part 1 – Articles (a / an / the / nothing)

### The decision tree

Ask, in order:

1. **Is it countable and singular?**
   - No (plural or uncountable) → often **no article** for general meaning, or
     **the** if specific. ("I like **music**." / "**The music** at the party was
     loud.")
   - Yes → go to 2.
2. **Can the listener identify exactly which one?** (mentioned before / unique /
   obvious from context / made specific by the sentence)
   - Yes → **the**. ("Can you close **the door**?" – the one in this room.)
   - No → **a / an** (first mention, one of many). ("I saw **a dog**." then "**The
     dog** was huge.")
3. **a** before consonant *sound*, **an** before vowel *sound*: a university (yoo-),
   an hour (silent h), an MBA (em-), a European.

### High-value rules

- **First mention → a/an; second mention → the.**
  "I bought **a** laptop. **The** laptop is great."
- **Unique things → the:** the sun, the internet, the government, the economy, the
  future, the same, the best, the first, the only.
- **General plural/uncountable → no article:**
  "**Cats** are independent." · "**Water** is essential." · "I love **coffee**." ·
  "**Software engineers** are in demand."
  (NOT "The cats are independent" if you mean cats in general.)
- **Jobs → a/an:** "I'm **a** developer." "She's **an** architect."
- **the** with: superlatives (the best), ordinals (the second), musical instruments
  (play the guitar), rivers/seas/oceans (the Ganges), same (the same thing).
- **no article** with: most countries (India, Japan – but **the** US, **the** UK,
  **the** Netherlands), meals (have lunch), languages (speak Hindi), most
  place-name streets, "go to bed/work/school/home", by car/bus/train, at night.
- **at home / at work / in bed / in hospital (BrE)** – set phrases, no article.

### Hindi-speaker patterns to fix

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| "I am software engineer." | "I am **a** software engineer." |
| "Give me pen." | "Give me **a** pen." / "Give me **the** pen." |
| "I went to the office by the car." | "...to the office by car." |
| "The life is hard." (general) | "Life is hard." |
| "He is best in the team." | "He is **the** best **in** the team." |
| "I have a good news." | "I have **some good news**." (news = uncountable) |
| "I did lot of work." | "I did **a lot of** work." |
| "She plays guitar very well." | "She plays **the** guitar very well." |

### Uncountable nouns that trip people up (no "a", no plural)

information, advice, news, furniture, luggage, equipment, software, hardware,
feedback, research, progress, knowledge, traffic, work, money, homework, staff,
weather, accommodation, rice.
→ Use "some / a piece of / a bit of": "**a piece of** advice", "**some**
information", "**a lot of** feedback".

### How to improve articles (the real answer)

Rules get you 80%. The last 20% comes from **massive input** + noticing. When you
read/listen, occasionally stop and ask "why *the* here and not *a*?" Over months it
becomes intuition. Also: read your writing aloud and let your ear catch missing
articles.

## Part 2 – Prepositions

### Time: in / on / at

- **at** – clock times, points: at 6pm, at noon, at midnight, at the moment, at
  night, at the weekend (BrE), at Christmas, at the same time.
- **on** – days and dates: on Monday, on 5th June, on my birthday, on the weekend
  (AmE), on time.
- **in** – longer periods: in the morning/afternoon/evening, in July, in 2022, in
  winter, in the 90s, in an hour (= after an hour), in a week.
- No preposition with: this/last/next/every → "**last** Monday", "**next** week",
  "**every** day", "I'll see you **tomorrow**".

### Place: in / on / at

- **in** – enclosed / areas: in a room, in a car, in a building, in India, in
  hospital, in bed, in the sky, in a book, in a queue/line.
- **on** – surfaces / lines / transport (public): on the table, on the wall, on the
  floor, on a bus/train/plane, on the first floor, on the left, on a street, on a
  page, on the coast.
- **at** – points / specific places / addresses: at the door, at the bus stop, at
  the station, at home, at work, at school, at the top, at a party, at the meeting,
  at 10 Downing Street.

> "I get **on** the bus **at** the station and get off **in** the city centre."

### Verb + preposition (learn as chunks – these have no logic)

- depend **on**, rely **on**, focus **on**, insist **on**, concentrate **on**,
  work **on**, spend (money) **on**
- listen **to**, talk **to**, speak **to**, belong **to**, happen **to**, refer
  **to**, respond **to**, according **to**, look forward **to**
- think **about**, worry **about**, care **about**, complain **about**, ask **about**,
  talk **about**, be excited **about**
- good **at**, bad **at**, look **at**, laugh **at**, arrive **at** (a building),
  shout **at**
- interested **in**, believe **in**, succeed **in**, result **in**, arrive **in** (a
  city), specialize **in**
- afraid **of**, proud **of**, aware **of**, capable **of**, consist **of**, get
  rid **of**, remind (someone) **of**, take care **of**
- agree **with**, deal **with**, cope **with**, argue **with**, familiar **with**,
  satisfied **with**, help (someone) **with**
- apologize **for**, wait **for**, look **for**, pay **for**, apply **for**, blame
  (someone) **for**, famous **for**, responsible **for**
- borrow **from**, suffer **from**, prevent (someone) **from**, different **from**,
  benefit **from**

### No preposition (Hindi speakers often add one)

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| discuss **about** the plan | discuss the plan |
| explain **about** it / explain me | explain it / explain it **to** me |
| reach **to** the office | reach the office (or "get **to**") |
| answer **to** the question | answer the question |
| request **for** something | request something (noun: "a request **for**") |
| enter **into** the room | enter the room |
| marry **with** someone | marry someone / be married **to** |
| contact **with** someone | contact someone |
| emphasize **on** | emphasize (something) / put emphasis **on** |
| comprise **of** | comprise / consist **of** |

### Practice (Week 4)

1. **in/on/at time:** say your full weekly schedule with correct prepositions.
2. **in/on/at place:** describe your route to work, every location with a
   preposition.
3. **verb+prep chunks:** 5/day, in your own sentence, out loud, → Anki.
4. From recordings, log every preposition error to the error log and drill that one
   for a week.
