# Minimal Pairs Drills

A **minimal pair** = two words that differ by exactly one sound: `ship / sheep`,
`vine / wine`, `think / sink`. Drilling them trains your ear to hear the difference
and your mouth to make it.

## How to drill (5 min)

1. **Listen** – hear a native say 8–10 pairs. (YouGlish, or search "minimal pairs
   [sound] audio".)
2. **Discriminate** – have a TTS or app say one word at random; you write which one
   it was. Aim for 9/10.
3. **Produce** – you say each pair, recorded.
4. **Sentence** – say the trap sentence 5–10×.
5. Next day: new pair. Revisit any you failed.

## `v` / `w`
vine–wine · vet–wet · verse–worse · vary–wary · veil–whale · vest–west · V–we ·
invented–in­wented(x)
**Sentence:** "The **w**inery makes **v**ery fine **w**hite **w**ine in the **v**illage."

## `θ` (think) / `t`
think–tink(x) → think vs sink/tank: thick–tick · thin–tin · thigh–tie · three–tree ·
thought–taught · path–pat · both–boat · faith–fate
**Sentence:** "I **th**ink the **th**in **th**read is **th**ree meters."

## `θ` (think) / `s`
think–sink · thick–sick · thing–sing · thought–sought · path–pass · mouth–mouse ·
worth–worse · tenth–tense
**Sentence:** "**S**omething **s**pecific: the **s**outh path is **s**afe."

## `ð` (this) / `d`
they–day · then–den · there–dare · though–dough · breathe–breed(ish) · other–udder ·
worthy–wordy
**Sentence:** "**Th**ey said **th**at **th**e o**th**er one is **th**ere."

## `ɪ` / `iː` (short i / long ee)
ship–sheep · bit–beat · sit–seat · fill–feel · live–leave · it–eat · pick–peak ·
his–he's · rich–reach · slip–sleep · chip–cheap · bin–bean
**Sentence:** "He'**s** living on a chea**p** shi**p** by the dee**p** sea."

## `ʊ` / `uː` (short oo / long oo)
full–fool · pull–pool · look–Luke · foot–food · should–shooed · could–cooed ·
book–- (no long pair, use) · stood–stewed
**Sentence:** "The cook took a good look at the cool blue pool."

## `æ` / `e`

## `æ` / `ʌ`

boil–bile · oil–aisle · point–pint · toy–tie · coin–kine · voice–vice

## `l` / `r`
light–right · lace–race · lead–read · glass–grass · fly–fry · climb–crime ·

## `n` / `ŋ` (sin / sing)
sin–sing · thin–thing · ban–bang · ran–rang · win–wing · ton–tongue · kin–king

## `p` / `f`

## `b` / `v`

## `s` / `z`

**Sentence:** "The pri**z**e i**s** a **s**urpri**s**e."

## `ʃ` / `s` (she / see)  and  `tʃ` / `ʃ` (chip / ship)

watch–wash · catch–cash · choose–shoes
