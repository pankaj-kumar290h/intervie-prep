## Why speaking to yourself works

You don't need a partner to build fluency. ~70% of the gain is available solo:
- **Self-talk / narration** builds the habit of forming English sentences fast.
- **Monologues** build the ability to organize and sustain speech.
- **Shadowing** builds pronunciation, rhythm, and chunk-memory.
- **Recording + review** is your feedback loop.

A partner adds the last 30%: unpredictability, listening under pressure, turn-taking.
Add that in Week 6 (tutor, language exchange, or a friend).

## The non-negotiables

1. **Out loud.** Whispering or "in your head" does not train the muscles or the
   retrieval speed. Actual voice.
2. **Every day.** Fluency decays fast. 15 min daily beats 2 hours weekly.
3. **Record twice a week.** You cannot hear your own errors in real time. The
   recording can.
4. **Don't stop to correct mid-sentence.** Finish the thought, then note the error
   after. Fluency first, accuracy in review.

## Where to speak when you have no time

- In the shower: narrate your day.
- Commute (alone / walking): describe what you see.
- Cooking: give a cooking-show commentary.
- Before sleep: recap your day in past tense, out loud.

These "dead time" minutes add up to more practice than most people's study sessions.
