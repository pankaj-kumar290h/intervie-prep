# Monologue Topics

60 prompts for **recorded 2-minute talks**. From Week 5, do one per day: think for
30 seconds, then speak for 2 minutes, recorded. Once a week, redo an earlier one and
compare.

## How to do a monologue

1. **Pick a prompt.** Don't over-prepare. 30 seconds of thinking, jot 3 bullet
   points only.
2. **Record. Speak for ~2 minutes.** Don't stop for mistakes. Use buffer phrases
   ([../05-fluency-confidence/filler-and-buffer-phrases.md](../05-fluency-confidence/filler-and-buffer-phrases.md))
   instead of going silent.
3. **Use a structure** so you don't ramble:
   - Opinion topics: *Point → Reason → Example → (counterpoint) → Restate*
   - Description topics: *General → Specifics → A detail/story → How you feel about it*
   - Past topics: *Set the scene → What happened → Result → What you learned/felt*
4. **Listen back once.** Note: (a) one grammar error pattern, (b) one pronunciation
   thing, (c) where you got stuck. Add the missing word to Anki.
5. **Once a week:** redo a topic from 2–3 weeks ago. It should be noticeably easier.

Target: Week 5 = fill 2 min with effort. Week 12 = 3+ min, few long pauses, some
variety in sentence patterns and vocab.

## Level 1 – Familiar / concrete (Weeks 5–6)

1. Describe your daily routine on a workday.
2. Describe your workplace / home office.
3. Talk about your family.
4. Describe your best friend and how you met.
5. Your hometown – what it's like to live there.
6. Your typical weekend.
7. A favorite meal – what it is and how it's made.
8. Your commute / how you get around.
9. A hobby or interest of yours.
10. The last movie or show you watched – summarize it.
11. Your phone – what you use it for most.
12. A place in your city you'd take a visitor.
13. Your morning vs your evening – which do you prefer and why.
14. A typical day at your job, start to finish.
15. Something you bought recently and whether it was worth it.

## Level 2 – Experiences / narrative (Weeks 6–8)

16. How you got into your career / field.
17. Your first day at a job or school.
18. A time you solved a difficult problem at work.
19. A trip you took that you remember well.
20. A time something went wrong and how you handled it.
21. The best decision you've made in the last few years.
22. A skill you learned as an adult and how you learned it.
23. A time you were nervous and how it went.
24. Someone who influenced you and how.
25. A mistake you made and what you learned.
26. A project you're proud of.
27. How your field / job has changed since you started.
28. A time you had to work with a difficult person.
29. The most useful thing you learned in the last year.
30. A time you changed your mind about something important.

## Level 3 – Opinion / abstract (Weeks 8–12)

31. Is it better to be a specialist or a generalist?
32. Should companies require employees to return to the office?
33. Does social media do more harm than good?
34. Is a university degree still worth it?
35. Should everyone learn some programming?
36. Is AI going to help or hurt junior developers?
37. What makes a good manager?
38. Is it better to rent or buy a home?
39. How much should work matter in life?
40. Are online friendships as real as in-person ones?
41. Should learning a second language be compulsory?
42. Is remote work good for career growth?
43. What's the most overrated technology right now?
44. Money vs. free time – which matters more and when?
45. Should we be worried about screen time for kids?
46. Is it better to have many shallow skills or a few deep ones?
47. What's one thing you'd change about how software is built?
48. Do performance reviews actually work?
49. Is "follow your passion" good career advice?
50. How do you know when it's time to change jobs?

## Level 4 – Explain something (Weeks 9–12, ties to interviews)

51. Explain how the internet works to a non-technical person.
52. Explain your favorite programming concept simply.
53. Explain a system you've built – what it does and how.
54. Explain the difference between two things you know well (e.g. SQL vs NoSQL).
55. Walk through how you'd debug a slow web page.
56. Explain what an API is, using an analogy.
57. Describe your ideal software development process.
58. Explain a technical decision you made and the trade-offs.
59. Teach me something non-technical that you know well.
60. Explain "caching" (or any concept) three times: to a child, a colleague, an
    interviewer.

## Rotation

- Weeks 5–6: topics 1–15
- Weeks 6–8: topics 16–30 (+ re-record 2–3 from L1)
- Weeks 8–10: topics 31–45 (+ re-record from L2)
- Weeks 10–12: topics 46–60 (+ re-record your Week 5 favorites – the contrast is
  your proof of progress)
