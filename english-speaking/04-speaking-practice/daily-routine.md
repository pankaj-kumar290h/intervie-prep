# The Daily Core (20 minutes, every day)

Do this every day of the 12 weeks and beyond. On top of it, add that week's Focus
work (~15–25 min) from [../12-week-plan.md](../12-week-plan.md). On a bad day, do
only this. On a terrible day, do only Block 2.

---

## Block 1 – Shadowing (6 min)

Pick 30–60 seconds of audio from your show/podcast (with a transcript if possible).

1. Listen once, just following along. (1 min)
2. Shadow it 3–4 times: play it and speak *along with* the speaker, copying rhythm,
   stress, and melody, half a beat behind. (4 min)
3. Last pass: shadow without looking at the text. (1 min)

Full method: [shadowing.md](shadowing.md). Reuse the same clip for 2–3 days until
it's smooth, then move on.

---

## Block 2 – Self-talk / narration (10 min)

Talk to yourself, out loud, in English, about a simple topic. No script. Keep going
even when it's clumsy. Pick one prompt per day:

**Week 1–2 (present):**
- Narrate your morning as you do it ("Now I'm making tea. The water is boiling...")
- Describe your room / desk / view in detail
- Explain your job to an imaginary child
- Plan your day out loud

**Week 3–4 (past):**
- Recap yesterday, minute by minute, in past tense
- Tell the story of your last weekend
- Describe the last movie/show you watched
- Talk about how you got your current job

**Week 5+ (opinions / abstract):**
- Should everyone learn to code? Why / why not?
- The best and worst things about your city
- Is remote work good for junior developers?
- A technology you're excited or skeptical about

**Rules:**
- If you get stuck on a word, say "the thing that..." and describe it, or say it in
  a simpler way. Note the missing word, look it up *after*, add to Anki.
- Aim to *not stop*. Silence-filling phrases ("let me think", "how do I put this")
  are allowed and encouraged.
- 2–3× a week, hit record for this block. Save the file. Listen back once that
  evening and note ONE thing to improve.

---

## Block 3 – Vocab activation (4 min)

1. Do your Anki reviews (or read your phrase-notebook), **saying each sentence out
   loud**. (~3 min)
2. Take today's 3 newest chunks and force them into 3 fresh spoken sentences about
   your life. (~1 min)

---

## Weekly additions (not daily)

- **2×/week:** record Block 2 and review it.
- **1×/week:** re-record a benchmark task (see
  [../progress-tracker.md](../progress-tracker.md)) OR do a 15-min conversation with
  a partner/tutor (from Week 6).
- **1×/week:** pick your worst recording moment and drill just that sentence 15×.
- **Daily:** one line in the progress tracker.

---

## What "good" looks like over time

| | Week 2 | Week 6 | Week 12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-talk length before drying up | 1–2 min, lots of pauses | 4–5 min | 8–10 min, few pauses |
| Shadowing | lagging, missing words | mostly keeping up | keeping up at full speed, matching melody |
| Recordings | painful to listen to | "not bad" | clearly you, clearly improved |

The discomfort in Week 2 is normal and temporary. Push through it.
