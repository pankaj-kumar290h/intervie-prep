# 05 – Fluency and Confidence

Fluency is not "speaking fast" or "never making mistakes." Fluency is **keeping the
conversation moving** – not freezing, recovering smoothly, and sounding like you're
in control even when you're searching for a word.

Much of feeling stuck is not a language gap – it's:
- translating from Hindi in your head (slow, produces unnatural sentences),
- panicking at a missing word and going silent,
- being so afraid of mistakes that you don't start the sentence.

These three files address exactly that.

## The mindset shift

- **Your goal is communication, not perfection.** A native speaker makes ~2–4
  "errors" (false starts, self-corrections) per minute and nobody cares.
- **Listeners are on your side.** They want to understand you; they fill in gaps
  automatically. They are not grading you.
- **Every conversation is practice, not a test.** The worst outcome of a clumsy
  sentence is... a slightly clumsy sentence.
- **Speak first, polish later.** In review you fix patterns. In the moment you just
  keep going.

If you internalize only this page, your speaking improves.
