# 07 – Interview English

This folder connects your spoken-English work to the interview prep in the rest of
this repo (see [../../interview-prep/](../../interview-prep/) and
[../../system-design/](../../system-design/)).

The interview problem for many strong engineers is **not** technical knowledge – it's
**explaining it out loud, clearly, under pressure, in English**. That's a language +
delivery skill, and it's trainable.

## Start this in Week 6

By then you have the foundations. Spend Weeks 6–12 doing:
- 1 technical explanation out loud per day (pick a concept from your DSA / system
  design notes, explain it to an imaginary interviewer, 2–3 min, recorded).
- 2 behavioral answers per week, recorded, using the phrases here.
- Weekly: one full mock (intro + 2 behavioral + explain one project) — ideally with
  a tutor or peer.

## The core delivery habits

1. **Think out loud.** Silence in an interview reads as "stuck". Narrate your
   thought process: "Okay, so my first instinct is... but that won't work because...
   let me think about..."
2. **Structure before detail.** State the shape of your answer first ("There are
   three parts to this..."), then fill in.
3. **Signpost.** "First... / The next thing I'd consider... / The trade-off here
   is... / To sum up..."
4. **Check in.** "Does that make sense so far?" / "Should I go deeper on any part?"
5. **It's a conversation, not an exam.** Ask clarifying questions, respond to hints.
