# Meeting, Standup, and Call Phrases

Everyday work-English. These are the situations where speaking confidently matters
day to day (and where interviewers imagine you operating). Memorize the ones you'd
actually use.

---

## Daily standup

**What you did:**
- "Yesterday I finished ___ and got it merged."
- "I made good progress on ___; it's about 80% done."
- "I spent most of yesterday on ___."
- "I picked up the ___ ticket."

**What you're doing:**
- "Today I'm going to ___."
- "I'll be continuing with ___."
- "My plan for today is to ___ and then start on ___."

**Blockers:**
- "I'm blocked on ___ – I need ___ from ___."
- "No blockers on my side."
- "One thing I could use help with is ___."
- "I'm waiting on the review for ___."

**Status:**
- "I'm on track for ___."
- "I might slip on ___ – I'll know by end of day."
- "___ is going to take longer than I thought because ___."

---

## Starting / running a meeting

- "Shall we get started?"
- "Thanks everyone for joining."
- "The main goal for today is ___."
- "We've got about 30 minutes, so let's try to keep it focused."
- "I'll share my screen."
- "Let's go around and ___."
- "Let's park that and come back to it." / "Let's take that offline."
- "To keep us on time, let's move on to ___."
- "Just to be mindful of time..."

## Contributing / making a point

- "Can I jump in here?"
- "Just to add to what ___ said..."
- "One thing I'd flag is ___."
- "From my side, ___."
- "I have a slightly different take."
- "Building on that, ___."
- "Can we go back to ___ for a second?"

## Asking for clarification

- "Sorry, could you expand on that?"
- "When you say ___, do you mean ___?"
- "I'm not sure I follow – could you give an example?"
- "What's the reasoning behind ___?"
- "Just so I'm clear on the next steps..."

## Disagreeing constructively

- "I see the appeal, but I'm worried about ___."
- "That could work. My concern is ___."
- "I'd push back a little on that – ___."
- "Have we considered ___?"
- "I'm not totally sold on ___, but I'm open to it."
- "Can we get some data on that before we decide?"

## Agreeing / committing

- "Yeah, that makes sense to me."
- "I'm on board with that."
- "Works for me."
- "Let's go with that."
- "I can take that on."
- "I'll own that and follow up by ___."

## Wrapping up

- "Let me quickly recap the action items."
- "So, ___ is going to ___, and I'll ___."
- "Anything else before we close?"
- "I'll send out notes after this."
- "Thanks everyone, good discussion."

---

## Video-call specific

- "Can everyone hear me?"
- "You're on mute."
- "Sorry, you cut out for a second – could you repeat that?"
- "I think there's a bit of lag."
- "Let me stop sharing so I can see everyone."
- "Feel free to drop questions in the chat."
- "I have a hard stop at the top of the hour."
- "Sorry, go ahead – you first." / "After you." (when two people talk at once)

---

## Written-to-spoken (async updates you say out loud in a call)

- "Quick update on ___: we've ___, and next we're ___."
- "Heads up – ___ is going to be delayed because ___."
- "FYI, I've ___." → spoken: "Just so you know, I've ___."
- "Circling back on ___ from last week..."
- "To close the loop on ___..."

---

## Drill (Week 10)

- Every day: give your real standup update out loud before the actual standup.
- Twice this week: run a mock 5-minute meeting alone – open it, make two points,
  disagree with an imaginary colleague, recap action items, close it. Recorded.
- Bring the disagreeing/clarifying phrases to a tutor session and role-play a
  design discussion.
