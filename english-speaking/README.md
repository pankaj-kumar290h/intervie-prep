# Spoken English – Self-Study Module

A complete, self-paced course to go from "I know grammar but freeze when speaking"
to "I can hold a confident conversation and explain technical ideas out loud."

Built for a non-native speaker (Hindi/Indian-English background assumed, but works
for anyone). No teacher required. You need ~30–45 minutes a day and a phone that
can record your voice.

---

## The core idea

Speaking is a **physical skill**, like playing an instrument or lifting weights.
You do not get better by reading about it. You get better by:

1. **Input** – hearing a lot of correct, natural English.
2. **Imitation** – copying it out loud until your mouth can do it.
3. **Output** – producing your own sentences, out loud, every day.
4. **Feedback** – recording yourself and comparing to the original.

This module is just a structured way to do those four things every day.

## How to use this module

1. Read [12-week-plan.md](12-week-plan.md) once. That is your schedule.
2. Read [04-speaking-practice/daily-routine.md](04-speaking-practice/daily-routine.md).
   That is what you do every day, starting today.
3. Each week, also read the 1–2 lesson files the plan points you to.
4. Every day, fill one line in [progress-tracker.md](progress-tracker.md).
5. Once a month, do the self-assessment at the bottom of the tracker.

Do **not** try to read everything first. Start speaking on day one with what you have.

---

## Rules that make this work

- **Out loud, every day.** Silent reading does not count. Even 10 minutes of
  talking to yourself beats an hour of watching grammar videos.
- **Record yourself twice a week, minimum.** It is uncomfortable. Do it anyway.
  Your ear is the only coach you have.
- **Accuracy later, fluency first.** For the first month, keep talking even with
  mistakes. Fix patterns later, deliberately.
- **Small and daily beats big and rare.** 30 min × 6 days >> 3 hours on Sunday.
- **Steal sentences.** When you hear a useful phrase, write it in your notebook and
  reuse it that same day.

## What you need

- A voice recorder app (built into every phone).
- A notebook or a notes app for new phrases (see
  [02-vocabulary/learning-system.md](02-vocabulary/learning-system.md)).
- One TV show or podcast you enjoy and will rewatch (see [06-listening/](06-listening/)).
- Optional but great: [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) for spaced-repetition vocab.
- Optional: a speaking partner or a tutor on iTalki/Preply once a week.
