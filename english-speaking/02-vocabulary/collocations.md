# Collocations

A collocation = words that naturally go together. "**make** a decision" is right;
"do a decision" / "take a decision" (mostly) is wrong. Native speakers store
language as these partnerships. Getting them right is what makes you sound
*correct*, not just understandable.

Most collocation errors come from translating from your first language. Learn the
English pair as one unit.

---

## make vs do (classic trap)

**make** (create / produce a result): a decision, a mistake, an effort, progress,
money, a mess, a plan, a promise, an appointment, a suggestion, a phone call, friends,
noise, sense, sure, a difference, a choice, an excuse, a point.

**do** (perform an activity / task): homework, the dishes, the laundry, a job, work,
business, research, exercise, a favour, your best, damage, harm, someone good,
nothing, something, an assignment, a course.

> "I need to **make a decision** about which course to **do**."

---

## take / have / get

**take**: a break, a shower, a look, a seat, a chance, a risk, notes, care, time,
part, place, responsibility, medicine, a photo, an exam, the bus, your time.

**have**: breakfast/lunch/dinner, a meeting, a party, a look, a rest, a shower (BrE),
a good time, fun, a problem, an argument, a chat, a baby, a headache, a dream.

**get**: a job, a chance, an email, a call, an idea, permission, a haircut, a cold,
a promotion, the impression, the point, better/worse, ready, lost, married, in touch.

---

## strong / heavy / high intensifiers (which one?)

- **strong**: coffee, accent, opinion, argument, possibility, smell, wind, chance
- **heavy**: rain, traffic, smoker, drinker, workload, accent (also ok), sleeper
- **high**: price, salary, standard, temperature, risk, pressure, expectations, hopes
- **deep**: sleep, breath, trouble, thought, understanding, recession, voice
- **big**: mistake, decision, difference, deal, fan, problem, surprise
- **great**: deal, importance, success, difficulty, pleasure, care

> "There's a **strong possibility** of **heavy rain**, so there's a **high risk** of
> **heavy traffic**."

## Work / professional collocations

- meet a deadline · miss a deadline · push back a deadline · a tight deadline
- reach a decision · come to a conclusion · draw a conclusion
- raise an issue · address an issue · resolve an issue · a pressing issue
- give feedback · take feedback on board · constructive feedback
- set a goal · achieve/hit a target · fall short of a target · exceed expectations
- take on responsibility · delegate tasks · prioritise work · manage your time
- hold a meeting · attend a meeting · chair a meeting · schedule/reschedule a meeting
- run a project · lead a team · report to (a manager) · work closely with
- gain experience · develop skills · build a relationship · make a contribution
- meet requirements · cause a delay · make an impact · add value

---

## Everyday collocations

- **heavily**: rely on, invest in, depend on, criticise, influenced
- **highly**: recommend, likely, unlikely, skilled, motivated, unusual, competitive
- **completely / totally**: agree, forget, understand, different, wrong, exhausted
- **deeply**: sorry, concerned, grateful, disappointed, moved
- **fully**: aware, understand, support, book(ed), recover
- **badly**: need, want, damaged, injured, behaved, affected
- **strongly**: believe, recommend, suggest, disagree, oppose, feel

> "I **strongly recommend** it." · "I **completely forgot**." · "I'm **deeply
> grateful**." · "We **rely heavily** on that service."

## Common wrong → right (translation traps)

| ❌ Often said | ✅ Natural |
|---|---|
| do a mistake | **make** a mistake |
| do a party | **have / throw** a party |
| say a lie | **tell** a lie |
| give an exam | **take / sit** an exam (teacher **sets/gives** it) |
| open the light / close the light | **turn on / turn off** the light |
| I have 25 years | I **am** 25 (years old) |
| discuss about | **discuss** (no "about") |
| explain me this | explain this **to me** |
| I am agree | I **agree** |
| take a decision | **make** a decision |
| put attention | **pay** attention |
| do a photo | **take** a photo |
| gain weight / lose weight | (these are correct – keep) |
| hear music | **listen to** music |
| tell about | **tell (someone) about** / **talk about** |
| reach to (a place) | **reach** (a place) / **get to** |
| return back | **return** / **come back** (not both) |
| revert back | **get back to you** (not "revert") |
| good in English | good **at** English |
| married with | married **to** |
| depends of | depends **on** |
| since 3 years | **for** 3 years |
| the whole day I worked | I worked **all day** |

## Where to check collocations
- **ozdic.com** or **just-the-word.com** – free collocation dictionaries.
- Cambridge Dictionary shows common collocations in entries.
- Google the phrase in quotes – if millions of native pages use it, it's fine.

## Practice (Week 7)
1. One section/day. Read pairs aloud.
2. Write 5 true sentences about your life using that section's collocations. Say them.
3. Any pair that surprised you → Anki.
4. Keep a personal "wrong → right" list from your recordings and corrections.
