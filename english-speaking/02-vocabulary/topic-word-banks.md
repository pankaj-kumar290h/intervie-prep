# Topic Word Banks

Ready-to-use vocabulary for the topics that come up most in conversation, small
talk, and interviews. For each topic: pick 10 words/chunks you don't already use
actively, add them to Anki, and use them in that week's monologue on the topic.

---

## 1. Your job / work

**Nouns:** role, position, responsibilities, tasks, workload, deadline, project,
team, stakeholder, client, deliverable, milestone, scope, priorities, workflow,
tools, stack, codebase, release, sprint, standup, retro, on-call, handover.

**Verbs/chunks:** be responsible for, be in charge of, deal with, report to, work
closely with, collaborate on, take ownership of, juggle multiple tasks, meet a
deadline, push back a deadline, get pulled into, be swamped, be on top of things,
fall behind, catch up, ramp up, wrap up, sign off, roll out, ship a feature.

**Describing it:** "My day-to-day is mostly...", "A typical week involves...", "The
main challenge is...", "What I enjoy most is...", "It can get pretty hectic when..."

---

## 2. Daily routine / lifestyle

wake up, hit snooze, get ready, commute, grab a coffee, get to work, wrap up for
the day, unwind, run errands, do chores, meal prep, work out, wind down, hit the
sack. "I'm a morning person / night owl." "I'm not really a morning person."
"I try to squeeze in a workout." "Weekends are for catching up on sleep."

---

## 3. Hometown / where you live

**Nouns:** neighbourhood, suburb, outskirts, city centre / downtown, local area,
landmark, cost of living, public transport, traffic, greenery, nightlife, amenities,
climate.

**Chunks:** "It's a mid-sized city in the north of...", "It's known for...",
"It's famous for its...", "There's a real mix of...", "It's gotten a lot more
developed over the years", "The cost of living is fairly low/high", "It's well
connected", "It can get really crowded", "The weather is pretty pleasant most of the
year", "What I like about it is...", "The downside is..."

---

## 4. Travel

destination, itinerary, book a flight, a layover, a direct flight, check in, jet
lag, backpacking, a package tour, off the beaten track, a tourist trap, a local
gem, hit the main sights, soak up the atmosphere, splurge on, on a budget, must-see,
a change of scenery, "I'd love to visit...", "It's been on my list for ages",
"I prefer exploring on foot", "We just went with the flow."

---

## 5. Opinions / discussion

"The way I see it...", "If you ask me...", "I'd argue that...", "There's a case to
be made for...", "On the one hand... on the other hand...", "It's a double-edged
sword", "It has its pros and cons", "It comes down to...", "At the end of the day...",
"That's a fair point, but...", "I take your point, however...", "I'm on the fence
about it", "I feel strongly that...", "I used to think... but now...".

---

## 6. Technology (general)

cutting-edge, outdated, user-friendly, intuitive, buggy, glitchy, seamless,
reliable, scalable, a workaround, a trade-off, adoption, a game changer, hype,
overhyped, a learning curve, "it's a bit clunky", "it just works", "there's a
steep learning curve", "it's been a game changer for us", "I'm a bit skeptical
about the hype around...".

---

## 7. Learning a skill / self-improvement

pick up a skill, get the hang of it, be rusty, brush up on, from scratch, hands-on
practice, trial and error, plateau, break through a plateau, stick with it, build a
habit, stay consistent, "it finally clicked", "I'm slowly getting there", "I've
been meaning to...", "practice makes progress".

---

## 8. Feelings / states (beyond "happy / sad / tired")

**Positive:** relieved, thrilled, content, proud, grateful, motivated, curious,
pumped, at ease.
**Negative:** frustrated, overwhelmed, anxious, drained, burnt out, gutted,
uneasy, fed up (with), stressed out, out of it.
**Neutral:** indifferent, torn, unsure, on edge, in two minds.

"I'm a bit fed up with...", "I was gutted when...", "I'm feeling pretty drained",
"Honestly I'm just relieved it's over."

---

## 9. Food

grab a bite, a quick bite, comfort food, a home-cooked meal, spicy/bland/rich/
greasy/fresh, an acquired taste, my go-to order, I'm not fussy, I can't stand
(coriander), I'm trying to eat healthier, we usually order in, a hole-in-the-wall
place, worth the hype / not worth the hype.

---

## 10. Weekend / free time / hobbies

"I like to keep my weekends low-key", "nothing major planned", "I'm into...",
"I dabble in...", "I've gotten into...", "It's a good way to switch off", "It
clears my head", "I lose track of time when I'm...", "I've been slacking on it
lately".

---

## How to use

- The 12-week plan assigns monologue topics. Before each, skim the matching bank
  here, pick 8–10 fresh chunks, Anki them, and use them in the recording.
- Reuse across the week: talk about the same topic 3 days running, adding chunks
  each time. By day 3 it should flow.
