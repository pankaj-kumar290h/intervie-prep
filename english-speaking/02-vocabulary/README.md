# 02 – Vocabulary

Your problem is almost certainly **not** that you don't know enough words. It's that
your *active* vocabulary (words you can produce instantly while speaking) is much
smaller than your *passive* vocabulary (words you recognize when reading).

The goal of this folder: **move words from passive to active.**

## The 5 rules of vocabulary that actually sticks

1. **Learn chunks, not single words.** Not `decision` → learn `make a decision`,
   `a tough decision`, `it's your decision`. You speak in phrases, so store phrases.
2. **Learn from context you met it in.** A word from a show you watched or an
   article you read has a hook in your memory. A word from a random list doesn't.
3. **Use it within 24 hours, out loud, in your own sentence.** 3 times. This is the
   single biggest lever.
4. **Spaced repetition for review.** See it again after 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2
   weeks. Anki does this automatically.
5. **Depth over breadth.** 20 words you can *use* > 200 words you *recognize*. Cap
   new words at ~5–10/day.

## Quick math

10 new usable chunks/day × 6 days × 12 weeks ≈ **700 new active chunks** in a
quarter. That's a visible jump in fluency. 50/day that you forget by Friday = 0.

## Where to get words

- Your show/podcast (see [../06-listening/resources.md](../06-listening/resources.md))
- Articles/newsletters you'd read anyway
- Conversations – when you *wanted* to say something and couldn't, look it up after
  and add it. **These are your most valuable words.**
- The lists in this folder, as a backbone
