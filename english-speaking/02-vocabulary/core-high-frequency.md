# Core High-Frequency Vocabulary

~85% of everyday conversation uses a surprisingly small set of words. If these are
fully *active* for you, you can express almost anything (maybe not elegantly, but
clearly). Master these before chasing fancy vocabulary.

Practice: pick a row, say the example aloud, then make **your own** sentence aloud.

---

## The workhorse verbs (know every common use)

| Verb | Common uses / chunks |
|---|---|
| **get** | get up, get home, get a job, get better, get ready, get it (understand), get tired, get in touch, get rid of, have got to |
| **go** | go to work, go home, go for a walk, go wrong, go well, be going to, go on (continue/happen), go through (experience) |
| **make** | make a decision, make a mistake, make sure, make progress, make sense, make money, make time, make a call |
| **do** | do work, do homework, do the dishes, do your best, do me a favour, have nothing to do with, how are you doing |
| **take** | take a break, take time, take care, take a look, take part, take place, take notes, it takes (2 hours) |
| **have** | have breakfast, have a meeting, have a look, have to (must), have a problem, have fun, have a good time |
| **come** | come here, come back, come from, come up (arise), come across (find), how come |
| **give** | give up, give a hand, give it a try, give me a minute, give feedback, give someone a call |
| **put** | put on, put off (postpone), put together, put up with (tolerate), put down |
| **keep** | keep going, keep in mind, keep in touch, keep an eye on, keep up with |
| **let** | let me know, let me think, let's go, let go of, let someone down |
| **need / want / would like** | I need to..., do you want...?, I'd like to..., I was wondering if... |
| **think / believe / feel / guess** | I think that..., I don't think so, I feel like..., I guess so, I believe so |
| **mean** | I mean..., what do you mean, that means..., it doesn't mean |
| **try** | try to, try doing, give it a try, I'll try my best |
| **use** | I used to (past habit), it's used for, get used to (adapt), be used to (be accustomed) |
| **work** | it works, work on (a project), work out (exercise / turn out ok), work with |
| **look** | look at, look for, look like, look after, look forward to, look into (investigate) |
| **help / show / tell / ask / say / talk / speak** | learn the pattern each takes (tell **someone** something, say something **to** someone, speak **to**, talk **about**) |

## Connectors (glue for longer sentences)

**Adding:** and · also · too · as well · besides · on top of that · what's more
**Contrast:** but · however · although · even though · while · on the other hand · still · yet
**Cause/result:** because · since · as · so · that's why · therefore · due to · as a result
**Sequence:** first · then · after that · next · before · while · finally · in the end · eventually
**Examples:** for example · for instance · like · such as · say (= for example)
**Reformulating:** I mean · in other words · basically · what I'm trying to say is · or rather
**Conditions:** if · unless · as long as · in case · otherwise
**Purpose:** to · in order to · so that
**Concession:** anyway · in any case · at least · to be fair

## Vague language (very natural – learn to use it)

thing · stuff · something like that · or something · and so on · and all that ·
kind of · a sort of ... · whatever · someone · somewhere · a bunch of ·
this guy / this thing / that place · you know · what's it called · thingy

> "We had some kind of issue with the... thing that handles logins, and stuff like
> that." — Sounds native. Perfect precision is not required.

## How to work through this file

- Week 1–4: one section per day. Read examples aloud. Make 3 of your own sentences
  aloud per section.
- Any chunk that feels unfamiliar → add it as an Anki card with your sentence.
- Weeks 5–12: come back whenever your speech feels "stuck" – the fix is usually in
  the workhorse verbs or connectors, not in rare words.
